# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 28, 2026**

## Overview

Word Connect World is an offline-first word puzzle game. We respect your privacy and minimize the data we collect.

## Data We Collect

- **Advertising identifier:** Google AdMob may collect an advertising ID to serve and measure ads. This is shared with Google as our ad provider.
- **Game progress:** Your current level, coins, stars, and found words are stored on your device. If cloud backup is enabled, this data is also stored on our servers under an anonymous device key — not linked to your name or email.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or location data. No account or sign-in is required to play.

## How Data Is Used

- **Advertising:** to show and measure ads via Google AdMob.
- **App functionality:** to back up and restore your game progress.

## Consent (EU/UK)

For users in the European Union and United Kingdom, the app shows a Google consent form on first launch. You may choose whether to allow personalized ads. Under the GDPR and UK GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, or restrict the processing of your personal data, as well as the right to data portability. You may withdraw consent for personalized advertising at any time in the app settings. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

## Children's Privacy

This app is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Data Retention

Game progress is kept until you uninstall the app or clear app data. Cloud backup records are removed after 12 months of inactivity.

## Your Rights

You can delete your on-device data at any time by uninstalling the app. To request deletion of your cloud backup, contact us at the email below.

## US Privacy (State Laws)

If you are a resident of California (CCPA/CPRA), Virginia (VCDPA), Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA), or another U.S. state with a consumer privacy law, you have the right to know what personal data we collect, request access to or a copy of it, request deletion, correct inaccuracies, and opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data. Word Connect World does not sell or share personal data for monetary or other valuable consideration. The advertising identifier used by Google AdMob may be considered "sharing" under certain state laws; you can reset or remove your advertising ID in your device settings to opt out of personalized advertising. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.
